# Reused components

Source: https://github.com/justsomeoneretarded/History-of-X/tree/18eee915526063977b1a73cc7cd34eb43dfce060

Retained: original five-chapter writing, Gemini REST and speech gateway, accepted chapter/audio checkpoints, transcript-assisted alignment and speech verification, licensed-media metadata, restrained soundtrack mixing, and final encoded-media checks.

Removed from this version: character rigs, limb/prop geometry, SVG scene generation, map/shape animation, animation directors and their repair workflows, Chatterbox/Qwen/Ollama dependencies, reference videos, old failed render experiments, and animation-specific approval scores.

This version uses a simpler archival editor. Image movement uses subpixel Cairo transforms; video excerpts retain real motion. No on-screen channel/source labels are added. Reuse rights and source context are preserved in credits and metadata.

The Ethiopia media and music bootstrap downloads only identified files at the pinned source commit and verifies SHA-256 checksums.

Download routing also adapts [Top10-Movies download_access.py](https://github.com/justsomeoneretarded/Top10-Movies/blob/main/movie_pipeline/download_access.py), its private temporary cookie helper, and [warp_proxy.sh](https://github.com/justsomeoneretarded/Top10-Movies/blob/main/scripts/warp_proxy.sh). The history runner pins wgcf 2.3.1 and wireproxy 1.1.3 release checksums, keeps transport credentials outside artifacts, and retains its own historical-media relevance and explicit-license checks.
